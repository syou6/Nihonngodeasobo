帰る vs 蛙: To go home or Frog?

Both words are read かえる (kaeru) — the exact same sounds. The pitch accent is the only difference, and it changes the meaning completely. Tap each card to hear the real native audio. 🔊

How the pitch differs

帰る (to go home) is HIGH-low — the voice starts high on the first mora and drops:

かえる

蛙 (frog) is low-HIGH — the voice starts low and rises, then stays up:

かえる

In a sentence

家に帰る

go home — 帰る 🏠

蛙が鳴く

the frog croaks — 蛙 🐸
