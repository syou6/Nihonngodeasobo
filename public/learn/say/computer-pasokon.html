How to pronounce パソコン (computer) in Japanese

パソコン means computer. It's read パソコン (pasokon). Tap to hear the real native pronunciation, then see exactly where the pitch goes. 🔊

Where the pitch goes

パソコン is Heiban (平板) — the pitch rises and stays high to the end — no drop. Each mora is shown high (高) or low (低):
