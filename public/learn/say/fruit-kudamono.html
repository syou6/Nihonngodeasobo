How to pronounce 果物 (fruit) in Japanese

果物 means fruit. It's read くだもの (kudamono). Tap to hear the real native pronunciation, then see exactly where the pitch goes. 🔊

Where the pitch goes

果物 is Nakadaka (中高) — the pitch rises, then drops after mora 2. Each mora is shown high (高) or low (低):

くだもの

L-H-L-L

Fruit in a sentence

果物を食べる

eat fruit — 果物 (くだもの)
