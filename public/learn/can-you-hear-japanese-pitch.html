Why you can't hear it (yet)

English is a stress language — we make a syllable louder. Japanese is a pitch language — the voice rises and drops at a specific mora. Your brain filters out pitch you're not trained to notice, so you literally don't hear your own errors. Minimal-pair listening retrains that filter. Related: why you sound foreign in Japanese.
