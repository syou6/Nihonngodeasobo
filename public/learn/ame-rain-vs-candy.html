雨 vs 飴: Rain or Candy?

Both words are read あめ (ame) — the exact same sounds. The pitch accent is the only difference, and it changes the meaning completely. Tap each card to hear the real native audio. 🔊

How the pitch differs

雨 (rain) is HIGH-low — the voice starts high on the first mora and drops:

あめ

飴 (candy) is low-HIGH — the voice starts low and rises, then stays up:

あめ

In a sentence

雨が降っている

it's raining — 雨 🌧

飴をなめる

suck on candy — 飴 🍬
