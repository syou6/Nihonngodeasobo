Every recording's accent type comes from the OpenJTalk accent dictionary. Then we measured each audio file's actual F0 contour and checked it against that declared type. Words whose audio didn't match were discarded.

FAQ

Honest answers

Do I need a microphone?

Not for Ear Sprint or Pattern Drill. Only Pitch Karaoke uses the mic.

Is my voice uploaded?

No. Scoring runs on your device with the YIN pitch algorithm. Your audio never leaves your phone — no account, no server, no cloud.

Do I need to know kana?

It helps. Words are shown with kanji and kana. If you can sound out ひらがな, you can play. If not, start with Ear Sprint — the audio does the work.

Is pitch accent actually necessary?

Honestly: you will be understood without it, most of the time, from context. Pitch accent is the difference between being understood and being understood effortlessly. It's also the part of pronunciation nobody corrects — this trains the correction in.

What is the audio made of?

High-quality speech synthesis, verified: accent types come from the OpenJTalk dictionary, and every file's measured pitch contour was checked against its declared type before shipping. 818 generated, 611 kept. It is not recordings of native speakers, and we won't pretend it is.
