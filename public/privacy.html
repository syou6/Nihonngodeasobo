Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 7, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how AMOR LLC (合同会社AMOR) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use NihonGo (the "Service") at amorjp.com.

1. Information We Collect

Personal information:

  • Name — for personalization and display within the app.
  • Email address — for account creation, login, and service communications.
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your card details.

Usage data:

  • Voice recordings and transcribed text from your diary entries.
  • JLPT practice responses and scores.
  • App usage patterns (pages visited, features used).

Non-personal information:

  • Browser type, device information, IP address.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI feedback on your Japanese language entries.
  • To transcribe your voice recordings into text.
  • To track your learning progress and JLPT level.
  • To enable diary sharing with your teacher (only when you opt in).
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To improve the Service and fix issues.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting.
  • Google (Gemini AI) — to process your entries and generate AI feedback. Your text is sent to the Gemini API for analysis.
  • Stripe — for secure payment processing.
  • Your teacher — only if you choose to share your diary through the teacher connection feature.

4. Data Retention

  • Free plan: Diary entries are stored for 7 days.
  • Premium plan: Diary entries are stored for as long as your subscription is active.
  • Guest mode: Data is stored locally in your browser only.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication via Supabase, and Row Level Security (RLS) policies to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage for authentication and app functionality. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
