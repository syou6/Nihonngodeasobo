Japanese Pitch Accent Quiz: 切る vs 着る (きる) — Can You Hear It? #shorts

【概要欄】
切る (to cut) and 着る (to wear) are BOTH pronounced "きる" — only the pitch accent tells them apart. Can your ear hear it?

✂️ 切る = high→low (atamadaka 頭高)
👕 着る = low→high (rising)

Answer: A · 切る ✂️
